## What's missing

The always-on guidance says how to *structure* a decision — context, options with pros and cons, a plain lean with its argument, a numbered pick-list — and says nothing about the language it is written in. So options arrive carrying the vocabulary of whatever analysis produced them, and the human decodes the framing before they can answer.

Measured in ordinary use: options offered as "a mandate on a mandate", "the diff-scoped proxy costs per verb change", "cost-per-subject". Each is a faithful summary of an analysis and none of them is the decision. The same choice in plain terms reads: "this asks for a test that checks the other tests exist — I don't think it earns its keep."

## Why it matters

A decision the human has to translate before answering is not a decision they can make. The guidance already holds that the reasoning is the deliverable and the container serves it; register is that same claim one level down, and it is the level at which the deliverable actually fails.

The failure is quiet. Someone who cannot parse an option asks for it again in plainer words, which reads as a clarification rather than as a defect in the offer — so nothing accumulates to show the guidance is incomplete.

The scope is every consumer, because the fragment is imported into every session.

## Resolution shape

One clause on the existing decide-one-thing-at-a-time bullet: write options in the plainest language that still carries the trade. Name the thing rather than its category, say what breaks rather than the class of failure, and keep a specialist term only where it is the subject of the decision.

It rides a sentence that already ships, so it adds no surface, and it is held at review like every other register rule — no mechanical check is possible.

Worth settling in the same pass: when a decision is offered as an interactive card rather than as prose. The card is right for a trivial either/or or a genuine side-by-side comparison, and a long question field in one renders as an unreadable block. Whether that guidance belongs in the same clause or stays a judgment call is open.
